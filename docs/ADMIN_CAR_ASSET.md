# Admin map car asset

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool, then downscaled for the map while
preserving its alpha channel. Project asset:
`public/images/tracking-car-3d.png` (200 × 300 pixels).

Visual reference: [Share Trip Reminder by Uber Design](https://dribbble.com/shots/4506553-Share-Trip-Reminder).
The car is an original generated asset, not extracted from the reference.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent car sprite for a live GPS map.
Create ONE original, premium photorealistic 3D white compact crossover car, isolated
on genuinely transparent background. Camera almost directly overhead, about 12
degrees behind a vertical bird's-eye view, showing a little of the rear bumper at
the bottom. The car's FRONT points EXACTLY straight toward the TOP edge,
longitudinal centreline perfectly vertical, no yaw, no diagonal orientation. Rear
bumper with subtle red tail lights at bottom. Rounded modern body, pearl-white
paint, dark panoramic glass roof, black windows, tasteful silver details, tyres
and two small side mirrors. Strong realistic volume through soft studio highlights,
subtle ambient occlusion and a small soft contact shadow immediately beneath
vehicle. This will rotate as a single sprite on a map, so keep a compact clean
silhouette and realistic anatomy. Entire vehicle visible with comfortable
transparent padding; car occupies about 88 percent of canvas height. Portrait
composition, symmetrical, centred on the canvas. No brand logos, text, road, map,
UI, scenery, ground plane, smoke, speed lines or extra objects. Refined realistic
3D product render rather than a flat cartoon or vector illustration.
